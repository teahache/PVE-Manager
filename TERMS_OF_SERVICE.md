## 1. Description of the Service

**PVE Manager for Proxmox** is an independent third-party mobile client designed to monitor, administer, and manage self-hosted **Proxmox Virtual Environment (PVE)** and **Proxmox Backup Server (PBS)** servers and clusters via their official APIs and SSH interfaces.

The Application operates strictly on a **direct client-to-server model**:
* All network communications occur exclusively and directly between your mobile device and your configured Proxmox/PBS host(s).
* The Developer operates no intermediate servers, proxy gateways, or cloud relay services.
* Server credentials (passwords, tokens, SSH keys) are stored encrypted strictly on your local device.
